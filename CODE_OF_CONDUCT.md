# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, and other conduct that creates an unwelcoming environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project, including issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other community activities.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through the repository's contact or reporting channels. Maintainers will review the report, investigate the circumstances fairly, and take appropriate action while respecting the privacy of those involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may use the following responses depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation:

1. **Correction:** A private or public warning requesting that the behavior stop.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning that continued behavior may result in restrictions.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary suspension from participation in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Permanent removal from project spaces when necessary.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from community standards and open-source Code of Conduct practices, including the Contributor Covenant.
